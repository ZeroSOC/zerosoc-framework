# ZeroSOC Trademark Guidelines

The ZeroSOC Framework and documentation are open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

Under Section 6 of Apache 2.0, the license grants copyright and patent rights, but **does not grant trademark rights**. These guidelines explain how the community, adopters, and commercial vendors may use the **"ZeroSOC"** name and logo to ensure the brand remains trusted, recognizable, and free from confusion.

---

## Guiding Principle: Protecting Project Identity & Preventing Confusion

The goal of this policy is simple: **we encourage everyone to use, implement, build upon, and commercialize solutions with ZeroSOC, while ensuring users always know what is an official project artifact and what is a third-party product or distribution.**

---

## 1. Permitted Uses (No Permission Needed)

You are warmly encouraged to use the "ZeroSOC" name in the following ways without prior written consent:

### Conformance & Compatibility
*   Stating that your software, platform, or service **implements, supports, or is compatible with** the ZeroSOC Framework.
*   *Recommended naming formats:*
    *   **`[Your Product] for ZeroSOC`** (e.g., *"Acme SOAR for ZeroSOC"*)
    *   **`[Your Product], ZeroSOC Compatible`** (e.g., *"Acme SOC Platform — ZeroSOC Conforming"*)

### Commercial Products & Distributions
*   Commercial software distributions, appliances, SaaS platforms, or solutions that implement the framework may use the naming format:
    *   **`[Company] ZeroSOC`** (e.g., *"Acme ZeroSOC"*, *"Apex ZeroSOC"*)
*   *Condition:* The offering must genuinely implement or conform to the framework, and the company or brand prefix must be prominent so users clearly understand it is a commercial offering from that vendor, rather than the upstream open source project itself.

### Commercial Services, Consulting & Training
*   Offering professional services, implementation consulting, training, or managed security services around the framework (e.g., *"ZeroSOC Implementation & Architecture Consulting by Acme"*).

### Community & Educational Use
*   Using the name in blog posts, technical articles, academic papers, presentations, books, and conference talks discussing the framework.
*   Organizing community meetups, study groups, or user groups (e.g., *"ZeroSOC Practitioners Group Milan"*).

### Linking & Unmodified Distribution
*   Distributing unmodified copies of the framework specification or linking directly to the official [ZeroSOC repository](https://github.com/ZeroSOC/zerosoc-framework).

---

## 2. Uses Requiring Prior Written Permission

To avoid misleading users into believing a third-party offering is the official upstream project release:

*   **Standalone Name Use:** Do not use "ZeroSOC" as the sole or standalone brand name of a commercial software product or company (e.g., naming a proprietary product simply *"ZeroSOC"* without a vendor prefix, or incorporating a company under the exact name *"ZeroSOC Inc."*).
*   **Official Endorsement Claims:** Do not state or imply that your product or service is officially certified, sponsored, or endorsed by the ZeroSOC maintainers without formal approval.
*   **Confusing Domains & Handles:** Do not register top-level domain names or official-sounding social media handles that confusingly mimic the upstream project (e.g., `zerosoc-official.org`).
*   **Altered Logos:** Do not modify the official ZeroSOC logo or combine it with your own corporate logo in a way that implies co-branding or official ownership.

---

## 3. Questions and Permission Requests

We want to support the ecosystem! If you are building a tool, commercial platform, or community initiative and want to check your naming or request permission, please open an issue in this repository or contact the project maintainers.
